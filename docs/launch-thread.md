# Launch thread (draft)

Tweets for the Qloo Agentic Hackathon submission. Each line block is one post; swap in the live URL and a screen recording of the walkthrough.

---

**1/**
I pointed an agent at an empty Dollarama in Toronto and asked for a live-music room.

It asked Qloo who goes out there, what rooms they rate, who they'd actually pay to see, then redesigned the real photo and let me walk inside.

Here's Openhere. 🧵

**2/**
The trick isn't the LLM. It's that the model never gets to guess.

Every decor, drink, artist and brand in the concept has to be a name Qloo returned for that exact crowd. Cite something Qloo didn't say and the agent gets it sent back.

**3/**
Then the part that made me grin: "Walk inside."

A real-time world model turns the redesign into a space you move through. WASD to walk, drag to look, switch to golden hour while you're standing in it.

One person. One afternoon. A room you can stand in.

**4/**
New this week: brand activations.

Type a brand and a headliner. Qloo measures how the two audiences overlap (with explainability, so you see who drove each pick), finds the co-sponsors both crowds already love, maps where the fans live around the venue, and the agent builds the booth into the room.

**5/**
Every run ends with "Why not just ask a chatbot?"

Same brief, no tools. Each of its picks gets checked against Qloo: in the top results for this crowd, real but not ranked here, or not in the graph at all.

The difference is the product.

**6/**
And for fun: /room.

Name five things you love. Qloo reads the taste behind them, an agent designs a room for the person who has it, the first track is already playing, and you can walk in.
